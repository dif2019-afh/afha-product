# AFHA.product

AFHA.product helps residential care home (Adult Family Home) owners locate high-quality caregivers and residents so they can fill their beds and keep a retained workforce.

## Who it's for

New Adult Family Home owners in Washington State (typically under one year of experience) running a 6-bed home with at least two caregivers.

## What it does (planned)

1. **Matched leads** – a list of qualified caregiver and resident leads, automatically matched to the home's characteristics.
2. **Negotiated Care Plan onboarding** – a structured, compliant process for admitting new residents.
3. **Caregiver training** – ongoing training so owners can trust the work their caregivers are doing.

## Constraints

- **Fake data only.** This project is not HIPAA compliant. Do not enter real resident or caregiver records.
- Washington State only.
- Simple web app that works well on a phone screen.
- Keep it lean: no complex UI, no unnecessary infrastructure or features.
